## PhD student: Lorenzo dei Rossi

#### Short vita

now: PhD student in the AILM group since Oct 2026

previously: BSc and MSc Chemistry in Padua

#### Projects

Plasmonic catalysis is a promising angle to transform our chemical industry away from a dependence on external energy sources and towards using
the sun as energy source -- may it be for energy generation or storage.
Lorenzo's main project aims to develop a seamless prediction of the catalytic efficiency in flow-cells that utilize the concept of plasmonic catalysis.
He develops a self-consistent approach that combines atomistic calculations with the physics at device level,
i.e., incoming light, reactant, and product flow.
His work takes therefore an important step from the white-board to industrial reality.
